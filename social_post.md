Built for #HackerHouseGoa with @TigerGraphDB: an agent that investigates card-fraud alerts on a transaction graph and knows when to ask before it acts.

It traces each alert through cards, device profiles, billing regions and 5,565 closed cases, then recommends policy-compliant next best actions with approval routes, before and after asking the customer.

Along the way it found two fraud patterns the documentation doesn't name: bursts of purchases priced just under $500, and one proxied phone used across 20+ cardholders' cards.
